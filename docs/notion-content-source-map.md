Public standard set:

- `Teal Core Standard v1.0`
  - Defines the three non-negotiables in observable terms.
  - Public wording should avoid code-first labels like `TR-CORE-1.0` except in technical/legal reference tables.
- `Evidence & Interpretation Guide v1.0`
  - Defines what counts as evidence, source examples, and public-safe disclosure rules.

Three public non-negotiables:

- Self-Management
  - Clear roles, decision authority, accountability, and coordination without relying on one central controller.
  - Evidence: role cards, circle charters, decision-rights matrix, consent/advice/delegated authority records, escalation paths.
  - Failure patterns: founder bottlenecks, hidden hierarchy, informal power overriding stated roles.
- Wholeness
  - Honest communication, emotional reality, conflict repair, learning, and accountability without punishment or performance pressure.
  - Evidence: conflict protocols, feedback norms, onboarding agreements, psychological safety practices, tension-resolution pathways.
  - Failure patterns: avoidance framed as niceness, informal exile, triangulation, retaliation, burnout culture.
- Evolutionary Purpose
  - Purpose that guides real decisions and can adapt as learning and conditions change.
  - Evidence: purpose-linked decision logs, strategy pivots, stakeholder feedback loops, retrospectives, change logs.
  - Failure patterns: purpose as branding, rigid dogma, ego-driven strategy, sunk-cost decision-making.

Public criteria from TRIOS:

- `EP-01` Clear purpose signal
- `EP-02` Sensing and adaptation loop
- `SM-01` Role clarity and decision rights
- `SM-02` Consent-based decision process
- `WH-01` Psychological safety norms
- `WH-02` Learning and development pathways

Public-safe language:

- Codes can exist in reference tables, but reader-facing headers should use plain names.
- Explain that partial signals do not prove Teal.
- Explain that all three are required for credible regenerative claims.

Content to publish:

- Separate tracks prevent confusion between organizational proof, practitioner competence, training completion, provider accreditation, and framework recognition.
- Organization/project track:
  - Aligned
  - Verified
  - Certified
  - Certified Advanced
- Practitioner track:
  - Trained
  - Certified Practitioner
  - Assessor
- Provider/framework track:
  - Accredited Training Provider
  - Accredited Implementation Team
  - Recognized Framework
- Guardrail: accreditation means a provider/program is authorized or mapped; certification means an entity or person met a standard.
- Training does not guarantee verification.
- Featured/paid visibility must never affect credential outcome.

Status labels to publish:

- Teal Aligned: self-attested alignment; no external review conducted.
- Teal Trained: completed education; does not imply verified implementation.
- Teal Verified: implementation reviewed based on submitted evidence.
- Teal Verified Conditional: active while specific gaps are addressed.
- Verification Paused: status temporarily removed while review occurs.
- Verification Not Renewed: prior verification ended or was not renewed.
